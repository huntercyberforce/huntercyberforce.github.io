About Hunter Cyber Force

Hunter Cyber Force (HCF) began its journey in 2022 after Farhan Chowdhury and Flash Forhad (Cyber Name), following experience working with different teams, decided to build a team of their own.

OUR JOURNEY

History

2022

The Beginning

Farhan Chowdhury and Flash Forhad started Hunter Cyber Force after gaining experience across different teams. HCF gradually received support and appreciation from its community.

2024

A Difficult Chapter

Following internal disagreements, the team's activities were closed. Pages, groups and much of HCF's earlier digital history were lost.

2026

The Return

HCF was restarted in early 2026, though regular operations were initially limited by other commitments.

August 2026

A New Chapter

Hunter Cyber Force began a renewed journey under Falcon Islamic Community, led by Flash Forhad as Founder & CEO.
