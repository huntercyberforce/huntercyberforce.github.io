01 / WHO WE ARE

About HCF

Purpose, discipline and community.

HCFMISSION · UNITY · KNOWLEDGE

More than a name.
A shared commitment.

Hunter Cyber Force (HCF) began its journey in 2022, when Flash Forhad (VenomX Flash) and Farhan Chowdhury set the team’s journey in motion. The team’s identity is rooted in cooperation, learning, discipline and a shared commitment to its community.

HCF carries the motto: “We Work For Islam And Bangladesh.” Its renewed chapter brings the team together with an updated leadership and training structure.

02 / OUR JOURNEY

History & Milestones

Every chapter shaped the team we are today.

2022
THE BEGINNING

Where it started

Flash Forhad and Farhan Chowdhury began the Hunter Cyber Force journey, establishing the foundation of the team.

2024
A PAUSE

An unexpected halt

Farhan Chowdhury brought the team's activities to a close in 2024.

2026
THE RETURN

First restart

HCF restarted in early 2026, although activities did not continue consistently at that stage.

AUG 2026
A NEW CHAPTER

Renewed under Falcon Islamic Community

HCF restarted again in August under Falcon Islamic Community, with Flash Forhad serving as Founder & CEO.
